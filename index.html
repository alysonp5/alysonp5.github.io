🎬 What Is Procedural-film?

Procedural-film is a remarkable piece of software that acts like a digital artist. You give it a topic, and it creates a complete, 30-second vertical film about that topic — all drawn and scored automatically using JavaScript code. No cameras, no actors, no video editing software needed.

Think of it as a magic box that turns words into moving pictures. The reference film shows the incredible journey of a monarch butterfly, from egg to caterpillar to chrysalis to magnificent orange-winged adult — all rendered in beautiful, code-generated visuals with a matching musical score.
